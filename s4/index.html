Walk-through viewer for rendered 360 images

Drag to look around, scroll to zoom. Click a ring on the floor (or a room below) to move. Everything you see comes from five fixed 360 renders. While you move, the two renders are projected onto the model's geometry, so walls and furniture keep their depth instead of zooming and fading.

How your files are used

  • 360 renders (equirectangular or cube faces) become cube maps at full resolution.
  • Camera file gives the exact position of each render, so every view starts pixel-identical to the render.
  • FBX model is reduced to a light proxy that the renders are projected onto. A depth pass per render hides surfaces a camera could not see, so nothing smears through walls.
  • Delivered as a standalone page and as a React component for Next.js, with hotspots, guided tour and preloading.

The house in this sample is a placeholder built in code; with your files the same viewer shows your renders.
